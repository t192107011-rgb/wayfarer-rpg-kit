# Soul arts · demo

Each bound soul now has three independent art choices with distinct combat roles:

- Radiance: control (Dawn Sanctuary), damage (Judgment Lance), enhancement (Dawn Aegis).
- Forbidden: control (Rift Well), enhancement (Blood Pact), damage (Blood Hell Burst).

The two original arts keep their IDs and behavior. The new third art is additive, not a replacement. Save protocol v15 accepts the new art ID while legacy snapshots remain limited to their original choices. Enhancement durations are shorter than their cooldowns: Dawn Aegis lasts 5 seconds with a 14-second cooldown; Blood Pact remains 6 seconds with an 18-second cooldown.
